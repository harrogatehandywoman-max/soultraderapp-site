SoulTrader Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

SoulTrader keeps everything you put into it on your phone. We don't have a server, and we never see your jobs, your customers or their details.

Who we are

SoulTrader is a job app for sole traders, made by Sarah Leslie in Harrogate, UK.

  • Contact for anything about your data: privacy@soultraderapp.com
  • Website: soultraderapp.com

We follow UK data protection law (UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025).

Your data stays on your phone

Everything you type or save in SoulTrader is stored only on your phone. We have no server and no copy of it.

That includes your business details, customers (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, notes), jobs, tasks, quotes, invoices, price list, stock, mileage, saved locations, survey measurements and photos.

  • Keep your phone locked with a passcode or Face ID, as SoulTrader's data is only as safe as your phone.
  • If you lose your phone or delete the app, your data is gone unless you've made a backup (Settings → Back up, restore & import).
  • A backup is a file you choose where to save or send. It holds all your SoulTrader data, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Delete the app and all its data is removed from your phone.

SoulTrader reads your phone's calendars only to show when you're busy. That stays on your phone.

SoulTrader has no adverts, and we don't sell or share your data.

This website

soultraderapp.com uses no cookies, no tracking and no analytics. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps basic server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. If you email us, we only use your email to reply.

Your customers' details

The customer details you keep in SoulTrader are yours. Under data protection law your business is in charge of them (the "controller"), and SoulTrader is just a tool on your phone. As we never receive them, we don't process them for you.

That means it's up to you to:

  • only keep what you need to do the job and get paid
  • tell customers how you use their details (your own privacy notice)
  • answer if a customer asks what you hold about them, or asks you to delete it (Customers → the customer → Delete customer removes them with their jobs, quotes and survey; invoices are kept, as you need them for your tax records)
  • check whether your business needs to pay the ICO data protection fee (ico.org.uk/fee)

Your rights and complaints

We only hold details about you if you contact us (for example, an email asking for help). You can ask what we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by emailing privacy@soultraderapp.com.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, tell us first at the same email. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you what we're doing about it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this policy

If SoulTrader starts sending any of your data off your phone, we'll update this policy and tell you in the app before it happens. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
