SoulTrader is the simple job app for sole trader tradespeople. Book the job, find the house, bill on the doorstep. No office, no paperwork pile.

Built by a tradesperson, for tradespeople

I'm Sarah, a handywoman and electrician in Harrogate. I built SoulTrader because every job app I tried was made for big firms with an office. This one is for when it's just you and the van.

Book a job

Customer, task, materials, date. Or mark it flexible and fit it in on a quiet day.

Bill on the doorstep

The bill fills itself in from your visits and materials. Show the customer, get paid, send the invoice.

Find the house

Save the location at the door. Next time, Navigate To takes you straight there.

Shopping list and stock

Know what's in the van and what to buy, by supplier, and charge it to the right job.

Quotes with your terms

Price list, quote, written yes from the customer, booked into your calendar.

Blinds and curtains
