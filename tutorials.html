Tutorials

Short screen recordings of the app, one job at a time.

Swipe for more →

Save a Lead

  1. Copy a customer's text or email. Tap the Lead bar on Home and paste it.
  2. Tap Lead. It's saved, and the bar glows while leads are waiting.
  3. Open Book a Job, then Leads.
  4. Check what was picked out: name, phone, address and the job.
  5. Tap Make It a Job. It's all filled in.
  6. Split it into tasks, one job per box.
  7. Put the materials in their own box. They go on your shopping list.
  8. Pick a day and time, then Book It In.
  9. Done. The lead's off the list, and the glow goes off.

Book a Job

  1. Tap Book a Job.
  2. Type their name, address and phone number.
  3. Tick how they get in touch, so you can always find them.
  4. Add each task and the materials it needs.
  5. Pick the day, the time and how long it'll take.
  6. Book it in.
  7. Pick where you buy each thing. Your shopping list is sorted by merchant.

Bill on the Doorstep

  1. Tap Create Invoice or Quote, then New Invoice.
  2. Pick the job. The bill fills itself in.
  3. Needs a price? Tap it and put in what it cost you. Your markup goes on top.
  4. Add your time.
  5. Show the customer, then take payment: card, cash or bank transfer.
  6. Paid. Email the receipt if you like.
  7. Paid jobs go to your Archive.

Mark a Job Completed and Paid

  1. Tap Jobs. On today's job, tap ✓ Completed.
  2. Tap Send an Invoice. The bill fills itself in from the job.
  3. Add your time.
  4. Tap Send the Invoice Now, and take what you used off your stock.
  5. Email the invoice from your own email app.
  6. It waits under Awaiting Payment.
  7. Money in? Tap Payment Received and pick how they paid.
  8. Paid jobs go to your Archive, with the receipt ready to send.

Send a Quote

  1. Tap Create Invoice or Quote, then New Quote.
  2. Pick the customer.
  3. Add your labour.
  4. Add materials from your price list.
  5. Not on your list? Type it in and add it.
  6. Not sure of the price yet? Mark it Estimate, to be checked.
  7. Put in what it costs you. Your markup is added on top.
  8. Estimates are marked on the quote, so the customer knows it's a ballpark.
  9. Write what the job involves.
  10. Tap Send. It reminds you about the estimate first.
  11. It goes from your own email, with the quote attached as a PDF.

Adding Your Materials and Markup

  1. Tap Materials, then ＋ Add item.
  2. Type the name.
  3. Pick the category and how you charge for it.
  4. Put in what you pay. Your markup is added on top.
  5. Tap where you buy it, then save.
  6. It's in your price list.
  7. Tap it to count how many you've got.
  8. Flip the switch to see what each one costs you.

Shopping List

  1. Tap Materials, then Shopping List.
  2. Tap ＋ Add Item. Type what you need and pick where you buy it.
  3. Tap it, then Assign to Job, so you know who it's for.
  4. At Toolstation? Tap Toolstation to see just their things.
  5. Tick things off. They move to Bought.
  6. All done? Tap Clear All.

Remove a Job

  1. Here's your shopping list, with the materials for each job.
  2. Job cancelled? Go to Jobs and open the job.
  3. Tap Remove Job, then Remove.
  4. It's off your jobs, and out of your calendar.
  5. Its materials come off your shopping list. Anything already ticked off stays.
  6. Your customer stays, ready for next time.
