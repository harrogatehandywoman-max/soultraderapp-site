Tutorials

Short screen recordings of the app, one job at a time.

Book a Job

  1. On Home, tap Book a Job or Task.
  2. Type the customer's name, address and phone, and tick how they like to be contacted.
  3. Write the first task and the materials needed, one per line. They go on your shopping list.
  4. Tap ＋ Add Another Task for anything else on the same visit.
  5. Pick the date, time and roughly how long it'll take.
  6. Tap Book it in ✓. Or Save, Book Later if you haven't got a date yet.

Adding Your Materials and Markup

  1. On Home, tap Materials.
  2. Tap Set up my materials (or Full price list once you have some).
  3. Tap ＋ Add item and type the name.
  4. Put in what you pay. The customer never sees this.
  5. Pick how you charge it: per item, per pack, per metre.
  6. Set your markup, or leave it blank to use your usual one. SoulTrader works out what the customer pays.
  7. Add where you buy it, then Save item.
