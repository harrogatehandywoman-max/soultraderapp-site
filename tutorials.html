Tutorials

Short screen recordings of the app, one job at a time.

Book a Job

  1. Type their name and address. Pin where you're standing, or search the house on Maps and paste the link.
  2. Tick how they contact you, so you can easily find them.
  3. Describe each task and add the materials it needs. They go on your shopping list.
  4. Book it in, or just save the job.
  5. Your materials are here.

Adding Your Materials and Markup

  1. Tap Materials, then ＋ Add item.
  2. Type the name.
  3. Pick the category and how you charge for it.
  4. Put in what you pay. Your markup is added on top.
  5. Tap where you buy it, then save.
  6. It's in your price list.
  7. Flip the switch to see what each one costs you.
